Choosing an interval

Fast clouds and traffic look good at 1 to 3 seconds. Sunsets and moving shadows work at 3 to 10 seconds. Construction and plants growing need minutes between frames. A 10 second clip at 24 fps needs 240 frames.

Gear that helps

  • Intervalometers
  • Sturdy travel tripods
  • Motion control sliders
