Crop or letterbox?

To deliver 2.39:1 inside a 16:9 file, keep the full frame and add black bars (letterbox). To deliver a true 2.39:1 file, crop to the size shown. Cropping 4K to vertical 9:16 keeps only about a third of the pixels, so frame for it on set if social cutdowns matter.

Sizes are rounded to even numbers, which most codecs need.
