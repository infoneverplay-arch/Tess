Reading the results

Depth of field is the zone in front of and behind your focus point that looks acceptably sharp. Wider apertures (lower f-numbers), longer lenses and closer focus make it shallower. The hyperfocal distance is the focus point that keeps everything from half that distance to infinity sharp, which is handy for wides and landscapes.

About circle of confusion

Each sensor size uses a standard circle of confusion. Projecting on a big screen or pulling focus on a 6K+ image? Pick a smaller value (the next sensor down) to be safe.
