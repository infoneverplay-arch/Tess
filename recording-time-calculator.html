How this works

Video file size depends on bitrate: megabits per second. Divide by 8 to get megabytes per second, and multiply by your recording time. ProRes bitrates scale with frame rate, so 4K ProRes 422 HQ at 60 fps is about twice the size of 30 fps. Long-GOP codecs like H.264 and H.265 use whatever bitrate your camera is set to, so check your menu and type it in.

Plan for three copies

Most productions keep footage in three places: a working drive and two backups, one of them off site. The calculator shows that total so you can budget drives before the shoot, not after.
